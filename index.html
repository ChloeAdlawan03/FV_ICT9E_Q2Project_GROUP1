O.B. Montessori Center


At OBMC, we provide learning from the Casa level, up until the Senior High School level. We are a pre-k to 12, integrated school with a unique Montessori way of teaching.


Our mission is to build future leaders. We aim to inspire and encourage stduents to step up and be independent, while also learning how to work with different kinds of people.


Our vision is to create a safe and comfortable environment for students to express themselves. The future we envision with the students that we nurture is one where the world changes for the better.


We combine technical learning through tests with practical activities to enhance childrens' daily life skills.
